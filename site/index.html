What's on the table

    Who's on my ballot?

    Enter your postcode to see every candidate in your district in ballot-paper order, who holds the seat now and by how much, and your upper house region.

    Fair by design

    Blind

    Option text never names a party, leader or slogan, and the order is shuffled for every person. An automated check blocks any change that would give the game away.

    Sourced

    Every option is a commitment the party has published, summarised from public trackers and party announcements. No position is guessed from ideology.

    Balanced

    Every plan gets the same format and tone. Reading lists mix left, right and neutral sources, labelled so you know what you're reading.

    Open

    Independent, non-partisan and open source. Anyone can check the data and report an error.

    Read the editorial policy

    Questions

    Will this tell me who to vote for?

    No. It shows which parties' published plans line up with your picks. Leaders, local candidates, track records and costings matter too. Treat your result as a starting point, not advice.

    Which parties are included?

    Labor, the Liberal–Nationals Coalition, the Greens and One Nation on every screen, plus 11 smaller parties wherever they've published a position. Independents don't share a platform, so the results page points you to the independents standing in your district instead.

    Where does the information come from?

    Policy summaries are based on the ABC's policy tracker and its guide to the minor parties. Candidates come from the ABC election guide, and postcodes are matched to districts using Australian Bureau of Statistics data.

    Do you store my answers?

    No. Your answers and postcode are saved only in your own browser, so you can pick up where you left off. If you use the AI chat, your messages are sent to an AI service to get a reply. Don't share personal details there.

    I found a mistake.

    Thank you. Please report it with a link to the source and we'll fix it quickly.

    How do I enrol or find where to vote?

    The Victorian Electoral Commission has everything official: enrolment, early voting, postal votes and polling places. Voting is compulsory for enrolled Victorians.
